I uncover the why, then design experiences that drive better outcomes.

Today I work with The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center's digital team. I started in graphic design, moved to marketing and media analytics, followed a dream of photography and landed in user experience fascinated by measurability, agility and attention to human behavior.

Selected work

Help surface insights for different types of users

Possip is an ed-tech platform that helps school leaders hear the voice of parents. After growing from 7 to 1,000 customers in three years, the product had been patched page by page. Over three months I ran the discovery, redesigned the landing experience and navigation around three personas, and tested it with ten people.

  • 25%fewer simple customer service requests
  • 15%faster onboarding for new customers
  • 50%less support effort for accounts with no data yet
